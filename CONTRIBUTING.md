# Contributing to OpenBOR Input Overlay

OpenBOR Input Overlay is an independent public project under the terms described in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md). Maintenance is limited to justified fixes for defects, security, and compatibility, as time and priority allow. New features are outside the current maintenance commitment.

## Report a defect

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/ijchavez/openbor-input-overlay/issues) with:

- The application version.
- Your operating system and architecture.
- Steps to reproduce the problem.
- The expected and actual results.
- Relevant logs, with secrets and other sensitive information removed.

For a potential security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting technical details in a public issue.

## Propose a change

Discuss the scope in an issue before implementing a pull request. Keep changes small and focused. Explain why the change is needed and what behavior you expect. Add or update tests when appropriate, and run `npm test` before submitting. Avoid unrelated functional changes.

Review the rights and license terms of any third-party resources before including them. Do not upload secrets, personal configuration, or generated binaries. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for the available project channels.

## Review and acceptance

The maintainer prioritizes relevant defects, security, and compatibility. A proposal or pull request is not guaranteed acceptance. There is no service-level agreement or promised review time. The maintainer makes the final decision to integrate or decline a proposal.

## Rights

Accepted contributions to the project's original code and documentation are distributed under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Contributors retain their copyright under the applicable terms; contributing does not assign ownership. Third-party material keeps its own licenses and notices. Consult [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md) and the [third-party notices](legal/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) before contributing assets or third-party material.

## Working together

Be respectful and professional. Discuss ideas and review feedback constructively, focus on the work, and avoid personal attacks or harassment.
