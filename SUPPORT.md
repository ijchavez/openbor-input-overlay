# Support

OpenBOR Input Overlay is an independent public project, not a commercial service with guaranteed support. Maintenance is limited and depends on availability.

- Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ijchavez/openbor-input-overlay/issues) for reproducible defects. Include the details requested in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Use [pull requests](https://github.com/ijchavez/openbor-input-overlay/pulls) for focused changes after discussing their scope in an issue. They are subject to review and are not guaranteed acceptance.
- Check [Releases](https://github.com/ijchavez/openbor-input-overlay/releases) for available versions. The historical public release is v1.2.5; no later release or release schedule is promised.
- Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities. Do not disclose them in public issues.
